# Monster token icons

From game-icons.net, licensed CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
Downloaded 2026-10-03 with Par's permission, as black-on-transparent SVGs (`scripts/monster-icons/svg/`). `scripts/monster-icons/list.tsv` maps each Tabletop id to its name, its game-icons.net path (author/icon), its D&D size and its creature type. `scripts/monster-icons.mjs` turns them into `icons.ts` here; the token art (`art.ts`) draws them light on a dark disc, with a ring coloured by creature type.

Authors: Lorc, Delapouite, Caro Asercion, Cathelineau, Skoll (https://game-icons.net).

Each icon's page is https://game-icons.net/1x1/<author>/<icon>.html, with the author/icon from list.tsv (the `source` field in `icons.ts`).
